Use case: ui-mockup. Create a polished, high-fidelity UX/UI concept presentation for PawTrack, a Spanish-language community app for tracking neighborhood stray dogs and cats. This is a proposed redesign, not a screenshot of an existing implementation.
Composition: wide landscape presentation board, three equally sized flat mobile app screens side-by-side, each fully visible top to bottom. Generous gutters and precise alignment. Deep midnight navy board. Top-left editorial title "PawTrack" with small subtitle "Propuesta UX/UI · Concepto". Above each screen label respectively "01 / INICIO", "02 / CERCA DE TI", "03 / AVISTAR". Small footer "Borrador visual · Datos de ejemplo". No hands, no perspective, no glossy phone hardware.
Visual language: preserve retro pixel-game identity through crisp 8-bit dog mascot, tiny pixel paw icons, stepped corners and selective pixel typography in logo or short headings. All paragraphs, labels, numbers, inputs and buttons use very legible modern sans-serif, substantially larger than tiny arcade text. Premium usable product UI, not a dense video-game HUD. Navy #10162b background, slightly lighter layered cards #1b2440, cream #f6f3e9 body text, cyan #57dce0 for primary action and selection, small lime #bee57b for success and XP. Subdued borders, no scanlines, no glowing body text, no magenta frames around everything. Consistent spacing and comfortable thumb-sized controls.
Screen 1 INICIO: header PawTrack and quiet bell icon without unread dot. Greeting "Hola, Ana". Compact profile card with an unmistakable cute GOLDEN PIXEL DOG head, floppy ears, cream muzzle, pink tongue and turquoise collar. Text "Nivel 1 · Novata", a clear progress bar labeled "5 / 10 XP" and "Te faltan 5 XP para subir". Large cyan primary button with paw/plus "Registrar avistamiento". Small descriptive subtext "¿Viste un animal? Ayuda a seguir su historia." Below section "Cerca de ti" and "Ver mapa". Two spacious animal cards with credible photographic thumbnails of a dog and a black cat, names "Luna" and "Milo", metadata "A 250 m · Hace 10 min" and "A 600 m · Hace 1 h". Quiet small achievement card "Tu siguiente paso" / "Registra otro avistamiento". Bottom navigation visible: Inicio, Cerca, Avistar, Códice, Perfil; Inicio active and Avistar slightly emphasized. No duplicate vertical menu.
Screen 2 CERCA DE TI: title "Cerca de ti". Search field "Buscar animal o zona". Filter chips "Todos", "Perros", "Gatos" and segmented toggle "Mapa" / "Lista". Main neighborhood-scale map with streets, a park, 3 animal paw markers, user-location blue dot and recenter button. Never show a whole continent. A bottom card for selected animal featuring real dog thumbnail, "Luna", "Perra · Dorada", "Vista hace 10 min · A 250 m", subtle "2 confirmaciones" line. Actions "Ver historia" as secondary and "La vi" as cyan primary. Same five-item bottom navigation, Cerca active. Maintain clear map area.
Screen 3 AVISTAR: header back arrow and "Registrar avistamiento". Short choice chips "Animal conocido" and "Nuevo animal", new selected. Compact step indicator "Foto — Ubicación — Detalles". A landscape photo preview of a friendly stray dog with a small "Cambiar foto" control. Location panel with miniature neighborhood map and success text "Ubicación lista" plus "Ajustar en el mapa". No exposed latitude/longitude numbers. Species chips "Perro" selected and "Gato". Form labels "Color principal" with value "Dorado", "Nombre (opcional)" with value "Luna", small collapsed row "Añadir más detalles". Anchored full-width cyan button "Publicar avistamiento". Do not crowd footer. Keep all content comfortably readable and evenly spaced.
Constraints: Spanish spelling accents correct, no invented product logos, no claim of implemented functionality, no rescue/adoption features. Showcase readable type, useful hierarchy, animal photography plus pixel dog identity. Beautiful but plausible shipping interface.
